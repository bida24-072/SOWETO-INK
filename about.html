THE STORY

From Soweto to the world.

Our Roots

Founded in 2014 by Sibusiso Dlamini and Ndumiso Ramate, Soweto Ink was born out of a desire to create a professional, black-owned tattoo space in the heart of Soweto. What started as a small shop has grown into the biggest black-owned professional tattoo shop in Africa.

We are more than just a studio; we are a lifestyle brand. We are dedicated to training African tattoo artists and expanding the culture of body art across the continent.

Expanding the Culture

The demand for our work has always been strong in Botswana. In 2021, we officially opened our doors in Gaborone, marking our first international expansion. This new shop allows us to serve our Botswana clientele directly and continue our mission of training local artists to the same high standards we are known for.
